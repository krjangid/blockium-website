Privacy by Design & FAQ

We believe privacy is an absolute human right. Here is our strict commitment to you.

🔒 Do you collect or transmit my browsing data?

Never. Blockium processes 100% of network rules, stats, and custom filters locally on your device. We do not track, log, or transmit visited URLs or user telemetry.

⚡ Does Blockium slow down my browser or drain CPU?

No. Blockium operates via Chrome's native Manifest V3 C++ engine. All background observers automatically pause when tabs are hidden, ensuring zero CPU and memory leaks.

🍪 How does the Cookie Whitelist work?

If a specific banking or e-commerce website requires cookie banners to work, you can toggle 🍪 ALLOWED in the popup to whitelist cookie prompts for just that domain.

⏱️ What is the "Time Saved" Metric?

Blockium measures the cumulative network bandwidth and seconds saved by preemptively dropping heavy ad scripts and skipping video commercials.
