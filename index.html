COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about Blockium installation, rules, and privacy protections.

Is Blockium completely free?
Yes, Blockium is 100% free with no paid subscriptions, premium paywalls, or hidden charges.
How does popup blocking work, and how do I allow popups on a site?
When an unsolicited popup is blocked, an overlay gives you options: "Open once" to open the destination once without whitelisting the domain, or "Allow here" to add the site to your popup exceptions. Because popup protection uses an isolated hook registered by Chrome, remember to refresh the page after changing popup permissions so the new rules take effect.
How does Blockium protect my privacy?
Filtering runs in Chrome. Settings, whitelists, blocked-domain statistics, and the recent activity feed stay in local extension storage. Public filter lists are downloaded periodically. Read our privacy policy for details.
What should I do if a website breaks or displays incorrectly?
You can pause protection for that specific website directly from the extension popup with one click. You can also toggle cookie notices to 🍪 ALLOWED, or submit details via our Report an Issue form so our team can update filter rules.
Does Blockium work on streaming video sites like YouTube?
Blockium includes helpers for YouTube, Prime Video, Hotstar, and SonyLIV that skip or fast-forward detected ads. Streaming sites change frequently, so results can vary. You can report a site when something stops working.
