Privacy by Design & Frequently Asked Questions

We believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Here is our exact commitment to you.

🔒 Do you collect or sell my browsing data?

Never. Blockium operates 100% locally inside your browser. Your browsing history, visited URLs, and personal information never leave your machine.

⚡ Why is Blockium faster than other adblockers?

Blockium uses native Manifest V3 declarativeNetRequest rules compiled directly into Chrome's C++ network core, eliminating JavaScript blocking latency.

🍪 How does the Cookie Whitelist work?

If a specific banking or login portal requires cookie notices to work, you can toggle "ALLOW" in the popup to bypass cookie auto-dismissal for just that website.

💬 How can I report a broken site or contact support?

You can report broken websites directly via the popup's "Report Site" button, or email our developer team at blockium.support@gmail.com.
