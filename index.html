Frequently asked questions

Common questions about how Blockium works, capabilities, and website compatibility.

Is Blockium free?

Yes, the core Blockium extension is free to install and use on Google Chrome.

Does Blockium slow down browsing?

No. Blockium is built on Chrome's native Manifest V3 engine, blocking unwanted ad and tracker network requests before they load to keep memory and CPU usage low.

Does Blockium collect my browsing history?

No. Blockium is designed to minimize data requirements. All rule matching and filtering happen locally on your computer inside the browser.

Does Blockium block YouTube ads?

Yes. Blockium includes video ad handling for supported streaming sites like YouTube to fast-forward unskippable ad breaks and dismiss ad banners where technically supported.

Can I disable blocking for a specific website?

Yes. Simply click the extension icon in your toolbar and toggle the main power switch to allowlist the current website.
