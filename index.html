COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about Blockium installation, rules, and privacy protections.

Is Blockium completely free?
Yes, Blockium is 100% free with no paid subscriptions, premium paywalls, or hidden charges. It operates entirely on open declarative filter lists.
How does Blockium protect my privacy?
Blockium performs filter evaluation directly inside Chrome using native declarative rules. Visited pages, browsing history, and personal inputs are never recorded, transmitted, or monetized.
What should I do if a website breaks or displays incorrectly?
You can pause protection for that specific website directly from the extension popup with one click. You can also toggle cookie notices to 🍪 ALLOWED, or submit details via our Report an Issue form so our team can update filter rules.
Does Blockium work on streaming video sites like YouTube?
Yes! Blockium includes dedicated streaming scriptlets that suppress pre-roll, mid-roll, and overlay ads, fast-forwarding them at 16× speed with instant mute so your content plays without interruption.
