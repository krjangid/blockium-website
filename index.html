Privacy & Technical Transparency

Clear, straightforward explanations of how Blockium works and how your data is handled.

Our Privacy Principles

Blockium runs entirely within your web browser. It does not track your browsing habits, does not sell user data, and does not require an account or login.

Permissions Used by Blockium:

declarativeNetRequest

Required to block network requests to known ad servers, tracking domains, and malware directly at the browser network layer.

storage

Required to save your whitelist preferences, visual zapper rules, theme choices, and local blocking statistics on your computer.

tabs

Required to check the active domain name so Blockium can calculate the site security rating and apply per-site settings.

webNavigation

Required to detect when Chrome blocks a navigation to a verified malicious domain and display it on your dashboard.
